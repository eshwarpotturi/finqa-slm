Financial statement analysis

A small model that works out figures from annual reports, and what it takes to improve it

The model reads one page of a bank's, insurer's or payment company's annual report and calculates what the analyst asks: a growth rate, a share of a total, a change between years. Questions come from FinQA, a public benchmark written by finance experts; none were seen in training.
